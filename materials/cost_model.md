# Production Cost Model

Status: initial framework

## Purpose

The project exists to help Cuban people who need communication during blackouts. A design is not successful if it is too expensive, too fragile, too hard to import, or too hard to repair.

This file defines how production cost should be tracked before any public build package is recommended.

## Cost Principles

- Minimize total working-system cost, not just antenna cost.
- Prefer ordinary materials and ordinary phones.
- Separate required parts from optional improvements.
- Prefer repairable parts over sealed or specialized parts.
- Prefer local fabrication when performance and safety are acceptable.
- Document cheaper substitutes and their tradeoffs.
- Do not hide regulatory or import uncertainty.

## Cost Categories

Track cost for:

- phone or reused phone
- router, access point, or bridge
- antenna materials
- coax and connectors
- passive coupler materials
- weatherproofing
- power adapters and DC converters
- mounting hardware
- tools required
- replacement parts

## Decision Rule

If two designs provide similar real-world connectivity, choose the one with:

1. lower total production cost
2. fewer specialized parts
3. easier repair
4. easier local substitution
5. lower import risk

Do not choose a more expensive design unless it gives a clear and necessary reliability benefit.
