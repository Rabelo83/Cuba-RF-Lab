# Bill of Materials

Status: initial categories only

Do not treat this as a purchase list. Exact models, prices, and sources are not verified.

Cost rule: every future production-ready bill of materials must separate required parts from optional improvements and must estimate the lowest practical production cost for a working system.

## Cellular Endpoint

- ordinary ETECSA-compatible Android phone
- ETECSA SIM
- charging cable
- protective enclosure if placed outdoors

## Data Transport

- USB cable
- USB power adapter or DC converter
- travel router or access point
- Ethernet cable
- optional Wi-Fi bridge hardware

## Passive RF Track

- aluminum rod or tube
- copper wire
- coax cable
- RF connectors
- nonmetal boom or supports where appropriate
- weatherproofing materials
- passive coupler materials

## Test Equipment

- phone field-test app or service menu
- NanoVNA if available
- measuring tape or calipers
- compass or azimuth app
- notebook or CSV logger

## Power

- solar DC source
- battery-backed supply
- fuses
- DC converters
- weather-resistant wiring

## Production Cost Fields To Add Later

For each final part, record:

- required or optional
- quantity
- local substitute
- estimated local cost
- estimated import cost
- required tools
- repair or replacement difficulty
- source and date
