# Passive Phone Coupler

Status: research plan

## Goal

Design and test a passive coupler that transfers energy from an antenna/feed structure to an ordinary phone internal antenna without active cellular retransmission.

## Fixture Requirements

- vertical phone adjustment
- horizontal phone adjustment
- adjustable phone-to-coupler gap
- adjustable phone rotation
- nonmetal support near the phone antenna
- indexed positions for repeatability
- locking once an optimum position is found

Initial gap sweep: about 2 mm to 30 mm.

## Candidate Geometries

- loop
- rectangular loop
- folded element
- patch
- capacitive plate
- inductive loop
- resonant parasitic element
- broadband coupler
- switchable 900 MHz and 1800 MHz couplers

## Measurement Rule

Do not assume the coupler works. Compare phone signal and app performance with and without the coupler at repeatable positions.
