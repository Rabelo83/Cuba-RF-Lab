# Glossary

## RF Terms

RSRP: Reference Signal Received Power. LTE signal power indicator, usually in dBm.

RSRQ: Reference Signal Received Quality. LTE quality indicator in dB.

SINR: Signal to Interference plus Noise Ratio. Higher is generally better.

RSSI: Received Signal Strength Indicator. Broad received power indicator.

VSWR: Voltage Standing Wave Ratio. Indicates impedance mismatch.

Return loss: A measure of reflected power caused by mismatch.

EARFCN: LTE channel number.

Band 3: LTE band around 1800 MHz.

Band 8: UMTS/LTE band around 900 MHz, depending on technology and deployment.

LPDA: Log-periodic dipole array, a broadband directional antenna topology.

Yagi-Uda: Directional antenna using driven, reflector, and director elements.

## Project Terms

Passive coupler: A non-amplified structure that couples energy between coax or an antenna element and a phone internal antenna.

Routerless architecture: A design using an ordinary phone as the cellular modem rather than a dedicated LTE router.

Blueprint status: One of CONCEPT, CALCULATED, SIMULATED, PROTOTYPE, FIELD_TESTED, or APPROVED.
