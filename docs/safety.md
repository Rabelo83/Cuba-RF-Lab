# Safety

Status: baseline safety notes

## RF and Legal Safety

This project is receive-focused and must use ordinary phones operating normally on the ETECSA/Cubacel network. Do not design or build unauthorized active cellular repeaters, amplifiers, transmitters, base stations, or interference devices.

Low cost must never be used as a reason to ignore safety, legal limits, lightning risk, battery hazards, or weatherproofing.

## Roof and Mast Work

- Do not work on roofs during rain, wind, lightning, or poor visibility.
- Keep antennas, masts, ladders, and tools away from power lines.
- Use fall protection when appropriate.
- Do not install anything that can fall into public space or damage nearby property.

## Lightning and Grounding

- Outdoor metal antennas and masts can attract or conduct lightning energy.
- Document grounding, bonding, and surge protection before outdoor installation.
- Disconnect test equipment during storms.
- Do not route coax or cables in ways that create fire or shock hazards.

## Water Ingress

- Weatherproof outdoor enclosures and coax connectors.
- Use drip loops.
- Avoid trapping condensation around phones, batteries, or USB adapters.

## Solar DC and Batteries

- Fuse battery circuits appropriately.
- Avoid undersized wires.
- Keep batteries ventilated where required.
- Protect USB adapters and routers from overheating.

## Field Testing

- Do not climb or adjust antennas alone.
- Log test conditions so results can be repeated.
- Stop testing if the installation becomes unsafe.
